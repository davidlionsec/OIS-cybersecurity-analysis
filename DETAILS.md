# OIS-cybersecurity-analysis
This is the cybersecurity analysis and penetration assessment I and my group ran on an educational institution with a running website.
We did a little bit of reconnaissance because that was the extent of the permission they gave us
We started with NMAP scan which is basically Network Mapping to see and discover three things. First are their open/closed TCP and UDP ports, second are the software services that the website is running, and then lastly are vulnerabilities to the open ports.
The tools we used are Kali Linux terminal, Wayback Machine and Nmap commands. 
Contained in the documents attached are presentation, risk register and word document detailing the vulnerabilities and it's scale, which we submitted to the organization and we were heavily compensaated for it.
